# 🚀 Viral Ad Copy & Hook Templates for ScroReel

Use these battle-tested hooks, captions, and ad creatives for your Meta (Facebook/Instagram) and TikTok ad campaigns.

---

## 🎬 Ad Creative Format Guide
- **Aspect Ratio:** 9:16 Vertical Video (1080x1920)
- **Duration:** 25–35 seconds
- **Structure:**
  1. **Seconds 0–3 (The Pattern Interrupt / Micro-Hook):** Start right in the middle of high tension or disrespect.
  2. **Seconds 4–20 (The Escalation):** Dialogue building up to a dramatic climax or forbidden touch.
  3. **Seconds 21–25 (The Cliffhanger Cut):** Cut right before the payoff/kiss/slap.
  4. **Seconds 26–30 (The Call to Action):** Big bold text overlay: *"Watch Free Ep 2–5 ➔ Link in Bio"*.
